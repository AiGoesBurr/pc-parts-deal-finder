Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This site displays third-party deals and product information. We do not sell personal data and we do not require an account.

Cookies & ads

Advertising is off by default. If you press Accept in the cookie banner, a third-party advertising script (Google AdSense) may load and may use cookies to serve ads. If you press Decline, the advertising script is not loaded.

You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser. You can also opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings.

Local storage

Your theme choice, watchlist, saved builds and compare selections are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not uploaded to a server. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Email

This site does not currently operate an email list or send price alerts. If that changes, this policy will be updated before any address is collected.

Affiliate links

Some links go to retailers. If affiliate links are added in the future, this page will say so. A commission never changes a ranking position.
