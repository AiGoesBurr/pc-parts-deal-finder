How we rank parts

The goal is simple: show useful performance per dollar, not paid placements. These rules explain exactly what the rankings do and do not include.

Sources

  • CPU scores: PassMark CPU Mark.
  • GPU scores: PassMark G3D Mark.
  • Prices and specs: public PCPartPicker listings and PassMark price data, refreshed by the scraper.
  • Deals: Slickdeals, filtered to PC hardware keywords.
  • Currency: ECB daily reference rates for non-USD regions.

What counts as "current"

Price rankings include consumer parts that are still relevant to a desktop build:

  • AMD Ryzen 5000, 7000, 8000 and 9000 series; Intel 12th generation and newer.
  • NVIDIA RTX 40 and 50 series, AMD RX 7000 and 9000 series, Intel Arc A and B series.
  • No server or workstation CPUs (EPYC, Xeon, Threadripper), laptop parts, or older GPU generations in the "best value" lists.

Older parts remain visible in the full category tables. Use the "Current & priced parts only" checkbox to switch between the full dataset and the current view.

Quality floors

  • PSUs: 80+ Bronze or better, at least 450 W, and known low-end lines are excluded from the front-page picks.
  • Storage: the front page shows NVMe (M.2) drives only; the full table still includes SATA drives.
  • RAM: 16 GB or more, DDR4 or DDR5.
  • CPUs and GPUs: parts must have a real price and pass the generation rules above.

Freshness

Each page shows when prices were last refreshed. If the data is more than 14 days old, the value rankings are paused and a warning is shown rather than presenting stale numbers as current.

Price-change percentages are hidden when the previous snapshot and the current price differ by more than 50%. A jump that large is usually a source or product-variant change, not a real market move.

Regions

Retailer links follow the selected region. Price data is collected per region on demand; if a region has no snapshot yet, the page says so instead of showing another region's prices in the wrong currency.

Deals come from Slickdeals and are US listings, so deal prices are always labelled in USD.

Even fresh scraped prices are estimates. Retailer stock and pricing change constantly, so always confirm the price on the retailer page before buying.

What we do not do

  • We do not accept payment for a ranking position.
  • We do not claim that a scraped price is live retailer inventory.
  • We do not send email alerts yet. The watchlist is stored locally in your browser and is checked when you open the watchlist page.
